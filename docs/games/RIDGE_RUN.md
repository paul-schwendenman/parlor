# Ridge Run

Ridge Run is a push-your-luck dice game for two to four players.

Roll three dice and choose one route whose number matches the sum of any pair. Advance one step, or two steps if you use matching faces. Stop at the summit if a double would go past it. You can climb up to two different routes per turn.

After advancing, roll again or make camp. Camping banks all your steps. If a roll offers no route you can advance on, lose your steps from this turn. Banked steps stay on the mountain.

The first player to bank each summit earns three points. Every later player to bank that summit earns two points. Each player can score a route once. The first to bank at least seven points wins.

Route heights for sums 2 through 12 are 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, and 3. Bots and disconnected stand-ins use the same actions as human players.
