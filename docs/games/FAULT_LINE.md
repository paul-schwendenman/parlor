# Fault Line

Fault Line is a six-round bluffing game for three to six players. Everyone starts with two faults, two silver caches worth one point, two gold caches worth two points, and four bounty chips.

Each round, choose a cache privately and offer zero, one, or two bounty chips. Bounty chips are spent when the offers become public. The caches stay hidden. Everyone then privately chooses one rival cache to open or passes.

Reveal all caches and openings together. Opening silver or gold scores its value plus the posted bounty. Opening a fault loses one point. Owners score one point per player who opened their silver or gold, or two points per player who opened their fault. If nobody opened their cache, the owner scores its printed value (zero for a fault).

Each cache is used once. Past caches remain public. The highest total after six rounds wins, with ties shared.
