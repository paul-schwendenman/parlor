# Harbor Dice

Harbor Dice is a Parlor roll-and-hold game for two to six players. A game lasts ten rounds. On each turn, roll five dice and choose which dice to hold before each of up to two rerolls. You can score after any roll. Fill one empty category even if it earns zero. The highest total wins, with ties shared.

A face category scores the total of the dice showing that face. The four other categories score as follows.

| Category | Requirement | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Twin docks | Two different faces show at least twice each | Three times the sum of the two faces |
| Lighthouse | At least one one and one six | 14, plus two for every additional one or six |
| Channel | Four or five different consecutive faces | 22 for four, 34 for five |
| Fleet | Four or five dice show the same face | 30 for four, 45 for five |

The scorecard shows each available score after a roll. Bots and disconnected stand-ins use the same actions as human players.
