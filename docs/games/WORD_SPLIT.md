# Word Split

Word Split is an original shared-letter word game for 2–8 human players in peer mode. Play three rounds. The highest cumulative score wins, with shared wins on ties.

## Writing

Each round starts with the same eight shuffled letter tiles for every player. Everyone has 75 seconds to submit English words of three to eight letters. Each tile can be used once per word, so a repeated letter needs a matching number of tiles. Tiles are available again for the next word. The server validates letters and dictionary membership, and each player can submit a word once.

Players can finish early to lock in their list. The round ends when the timer expires or every seat is ready. While writing, each player sees their own accepted words, the rack, the countdown, cumulative scores, and who is ready. Spectators see the public information.

## Reveal

All words and their authors are shown. A word submitted by one player earns them one point per letter. A word submitted by multiple players earns zero. After 12 seconds the next rack opens. After the third reveal, the results and final standings remain visible.

A disconnected player can rejoin before the deadline and continue with their submitted list intact. A permanently removed seat keeps previously earned points and submitted words, and counts as ready for the remainder of the game.

The English dictionary comes from [word-list](https://github.com/sindresorhus/word-list) and is loaded by the server.
