# Council Ledger

Council Ledger is a bluffing game for two to six players. Everyone starts with three coins and zero standing. At the start of your turn you get one private permit chosen at random. You can claim any permit or choose public service to earn one coin and one standing.

| Claim | Effect when accepted |
|---|---|
| Contracts | Earn four coins |
| Public works | Spend two coins to earn three standing |
| Outreach | Earn one coin and two standing |

A claim opens an eight-second response window. Rivals with a coin can challenge or stand. A timeout counts as standing. All choices resolve together. If nobody challenges, apply the claim and keep the permit private. If anyone challenges, show the permit. A matching permit lets the claim through, and each challenger loses one coin. A bluff cancels the claim, each challenger gains one standing, and the proposer loses up to one coin.

The game ends after resolving a turn when anyone reaches 12 standing or after eight complete rounds. The highest standing wins, with ties shared.
