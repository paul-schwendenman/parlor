# Night Market

Night Market is a simultaneous drafting game for two to five players. Play three seasons with four picks each.

At the start of a season, each player gets five goods. Pick one privately and decide whether to sell it for the printed coins or display it in your stall. Reveal the picks together and pass the goods that remain. Passing changes direction each season. Discard the final unpicked good.

At the end of a season, score one point per coin and one per displayed good. Each lantern and silk pair earns five extra points, as does each tea and pottery pair. Each displayed spice earns one extra point per distinct non-spice kind in that stall. Then reset coins and stalls. The highest total across all three seasons wins, with ties shared.
